Nutrition Insights

Educational guidance on key pillars of balanced nutrition.

💪
Protein

Protein is essential for building and repairing tissues, supporting immunity, and making enzymes. Aim for a variety of sources such as legumes, eggs, fish, dairy, or meat.

🌾
Fiber

Dietary fiber supports gut health, blood sugar balance, and satiety. Most adults benefit from 25–38 g per day from vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains.

💧
Hydration

Water is involved in virtually every body function. General guidance suggests around 2–3 litres daily, varying with activity, climate, and individual factors.

🥬
Fruits & Vegetables

Fruits and vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and fiber. A colourful variety — "eating the rainbow" — helps ensure broad micronutrient coverage.

⚖️
Meal Balance

A balanced plate typically includes a protein source, complex carbohydrate, healthy fat, and plenty of vegetables. Sustainable eating beats short-term restriction.

🌞
Healthy Fats

Unsaturated fats from sources like avocados, nuts, seeds, and oily fish support heart health and fat-soluble vitamin absorption. Fat is not the enemy — quality matters.
